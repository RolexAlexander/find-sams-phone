Find Sam’s Phone

Sam dropped his phone somewhere in this giant maze. You can’t see it, but you can hear it.

  • 1Turn your sound on. The ringtone gets louder the closer you are.
  • 2Watch the meter at the top: Louder ▲ means that step helped, Quieter ▼ means go back.
  • 3Move with the arrow keys / WASD, the on-screen buttons, or swipe.
  • 4Find the phone in 60 seconds and you’ll get a code. Type it in the chat!
